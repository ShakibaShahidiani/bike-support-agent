# Warranty

*Fictional policy for demo. Ferro Cycles is not a real company.*

- Frames: **5 years** against manufacturing defects for the original owner.
- Forks, wheels and components: **2 years**.
- E-bike batteries and motors (Ridge E): **2 years** or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
- Not covered: crash damage, normal wear (tyres, brake pads, chains), and modifications.
- Any frame damage or battery problem must be inspected by our service team. Stop riding and contact support.
