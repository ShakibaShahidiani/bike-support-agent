# Returns policy (current)

*Fictional policy for demo. Ferro Cycles is not a real company.*

- You can return an unused bike within **30 days** of delivery.
- Return shipping is **free**: we send a prepaid label and arrange pickup.
- Bikes must be returned in the original box with all parts, with less than 50 km ridden.
- Refunds are processed after inspection at our warehouse. A support agent confirms the outcome;
  this assistant cannot promise refund amounts or dates.
- To start a return, give us your order number (format CN-1234) and a short reason.
