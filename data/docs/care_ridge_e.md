# Care guide: Ridge E

*Fictional policy for demo. Ferro Cycles is not a real company.*

- Charge the battery indoors at room temperature with the original charger only.
- For storage longer than a month, keep the battery at 30 to 60 percent charge.
- Never use a pressure washer on the motor or battery.
- Check brake pads every 500 km; e-MTBs wear pads faster because of their weight.
