# General bike care

*Fictional policy for demo. Ferro Cycles is not a real company.*

- Clean and lube the chain every 200 km, or after riding in rain.
- Check tyre pressure weekly; the range is printed on the tyre sidewall.
- Thru-axles should be tightened to the torque printed on the axle.
- Have the bike serviced by a workshop once a year.
